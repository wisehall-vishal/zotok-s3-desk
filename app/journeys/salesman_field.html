Phase I
Attendance & Retailer Master
Problem
  • No valid retailer data across Odisha & Tamil Nadu GT
  • No visibility of visits, check-ins or clock-ins
  • Ad hoc WA groups + Excel float around with zero tracking
Solution
  • Retailer master is built from field capture — photo, geotag, name, phone — not assumed to pre-exist
  • Clock-in / check-in / clock-out on {{BRAND}}'s own WABA gives the ASM a live map of who's where
  • One direct thread per salesman replaces the WA group + Excel mess
Assumption
  • Salesman carries a smartphone with GPS + camera + WhatsApp
  • Distributor/ASM supplies phone numbers upfront — no self-signup, since {{BRAND}} has no owned sales team here
  • Retailer master starts near-empty in Odisha/TN by design
Phase II
Van Sales & Secondary Sales
Problem
  • No visibility of secondary sales — van sales / retailer-wise orders
  • No visibility of outlet quality — productivity or SKU mix
Solution
  • Every van-sale order — handwritten chit or catalog tap — is captured on WhatsApp and structured against retailer + SKU
  • SKU-wise roll-up per retailer surfaces outlet productivity automatically, for the first time
Assumption
  • Van carries a fixed opening stock declared at day-start — no warehouse ERP integration assumed for pilot
  • Order capture works whether the retailer writes it on paper or the salesman taps a catalog — no single format is forced
Phase III
Tasks, Market Intel & Incentives
Problem
  • No mechanism to implement KRA/KPI, tasks or checklists
  • No mechanism to track market intelligence, schemes or retailer engagement
Solution
  • Tasks/checklists pushed and closed on the same WABA thread the salesman already lives in — no separate app
  • Competition notes (pricing, schemes, stock-outs) captured as structured photo+text at the point of visit
Assumption
  • KRA/KPI definitions come from {{BRAND}}/distributor leadership — this journey shows the capture mechanism, not the policy
  • Competitive intel is salesman-reported at this stage, not automated shelf-share detection
